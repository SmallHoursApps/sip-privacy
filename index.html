Sip — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Sip is a hydration tracker for Garmin watches, published by Small Hours Apps (smallhourslabs@gmail.com).

What Sip stores

Your drink log, daily totals, goal, containers and settings. All of this is stored only on your watch, plus the settings Garmin syncs through the Garmin Connect / Connect IQ app.

What Sip collects

Nothing. Sip makes no network requests, has no analytics or ads, and sends no data to the publisher or any third party. It reads Garmin's own on-watch weather only if you turn on "Heat boost", and that never leaves the watch.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Sip from your watch removes all of its data.

Health note

Sip is a habit tool, not medical advice. Listen to your body and follow your doctor's advice about how much to drink.
